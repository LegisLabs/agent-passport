Register an AI product

A registration is a filing, not an approval. You record who you are, who is accountable, your insurance, and Independent Assurance Evidence that the product meets the minimum assurance requirements for its use case. Nobody reviews it; it must be present, complete and declared accurate. Banks then decide for themselves whether to admit the product to their lists.

The register does not certify that AI agents are good. It guarantees that someone is accountable when they are bad.
