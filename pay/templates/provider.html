Register an AI product

The bank registers each AI product before it approves it for its customers. The entry records the provider's identity, who is accountable, its insurance, the payment intent, its UK data protection declaration, and Independent Assurance Evidence that the product meets the minimum assurance requirements for its use case. Seven completeness checks run and are recorded; the bank then approves the product for its list, with ceilings and a hold condition, or not.
