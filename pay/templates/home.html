The shift

Agents are already buying. Last holiday season AI and agents stood behind a fifth of everything sold online, and the businesses behind those shoppers are handing over their invoices next. The question is no longer whether agents will pay, but on whose authority.

Sources: Salesforce holiday shopping data, 2024 and 2025; Juniper Research, agentic commerce spend of $1.5 trillion in 2030 and 1.3 billion people buying through agents by 2031.

The numbers

Payment fraud is already an industry. £1.28 billion was taken from UK customers last year, and every pound of it was authorised by someone who believed what they were shown.

Source: UK Finance, Annual Fraud Report 2026. Losses reported to UK Finance.

The problem

An agent believes what it is shown. Give it the right document and it will pay the wrong account with perfect confidence, at machine speed, without a second look.

Identity alone answers none of this. Knowing who an agent is tells you nothing about what it may do.

The standard

We think the answer is not to watch agents more closely. It is to give them a place in the system: a passport that carries an authority's approval, a customer's signature and a bank's verdict, so that for the first time in history an AI model becomes an official actor with a defined, provable, revocable authority.

  • An authority approves a model once and sets the ceilings every mandate on it must respect. It never reviews a customer's limits and never sees a customer's data.
  • A customer signs the mandate: whom the agent may pay, how much, until when. The passport is issued the moment it is signed, within the ceilings, live at once.
  • A bank asks nine questions of every payment: the authority's signature, the registry, the agent's key, the mandate, the payee, the limits, the supervisor's condition. In order, deny by default, with a signed receipt.
  • Every decision can be replayed. Refusals reach the authority. Two on the same rule in a day raise an alert. Suspension or revocation takes effect on the next instruction, on every rail.
