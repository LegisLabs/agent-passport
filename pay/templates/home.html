vouch.finance

Built on vouch.finance

A passport is a promise. vouch.finance is where the promise becomes money that can only be spent the way it was promised. Every mandate a customer signs is minted as an AI Voucher on the vouch rail: purpose-bound value that carries the agent's limits, its payees and its passport identifier.

  • Minted at signing. The voucher is created the moment the passport is issued, with the customer's limits as its policy.
  • Two rails, one answer. The bank runs the nine checks; the vouch policy engine authorises the settlement. A refusal on one is a refusal on both.
  • Revoked in seconds. Suspend or revoke a passport and the voucher is revoked with it. The next instruction fails on every rail.
  • Replayed against the kits. The kya-licence and agent-mandate kits replay through the same engine, with every decline a cited rule.
