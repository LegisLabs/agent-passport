How it works

  1. A provider registers a model

    Once, with pinned version and attested documentation. Provider Panel

  2. The authority approves it with ceilings

    Automated checks, a six-step Standards Review Assistant, a case officer's decision. Regulator Panel

  3. A customer registers an agent and signs its mandate

    Which suppliers, how much, until when. The passport is issued at signing. Customer Panel

  4. The bank verifies every instruction

    Nine checks, deny by default, a signed receipt. A manipulated invoice is refused before payment. Action Terminal

  5. Refusals feed back to the authority

    Two on the same rule in a day raise an alert. Suspend, investigate, reinstate or revoke. Audit
