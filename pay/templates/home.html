The shift

Agents are already buying. Last holiday season AI and agents stood behind a fifth of everything sold online, and the businesses behind those shoppers are handing over their invoices next. The question is no longer whether agents will pay, but on whose authority.

Agentic commerce, global, $ billion: actual to 2025, forecast to 2030

2024 and 2025: online holiday sales influenced by AI and agents, Salesforce 2024, Salesforce 2025. 2030: agentic commerce spend, Juniper Research forecast; McKinsey puts the same year at $3 to 5 trillion, and Juniper expects 1.3 billion people buying through agents by 2031.

The numbers

Payment fraud is already an industry. Of the £1.28 billion taken from UK customers last year, £576 million was authorised push payment fraud, up 19% in a year: payments the victims made themselves because they believed what they were shown. It is the one kind of fraud an agent can commit on your behalf.

The problem

An agent believes what it is shown. Give it the right document and it will pay the wrong account with perfect confidence, at machine speed, without a second look.

Identity alone answers none of this. Knowing who an agent is tells you nothing about what it may do.

The standard

We think the answer is not to watch agents more closely, but to give them a place in the system: an authority's approval, a customer's signature, a bank's verdict. For the first time, an AI model becomes an official actor with a defined, provable, revocable authority.

An authority approves a model once and sets the ceilings every mandate must respect. A customer signs the mandate: whom the agent may pay, how much, until when, and the passport is issued at once. A bank asks nine questions of every payment, in order, deny by default, with a signed receipt. Every decision can be replayed, every refusal reaches the authority, and revocation takes effect on the next instruction.

Our proposal

One approval, one signature, nine questions, and a receipt. No new licence for every agent. No review of anyone's invoices.

  1. The provider registers a model

    Once. Pinned version, attested documentation.

  2. The authority approves it

    Once. It sets the ceilings every mandate must respect, and one condition.

  3. The customer signs the mandate

    Whom the agent may pay, how much, until when. The passport is issued at once.

  4. The bank checks every payment

    Nine questions, in order, deny by default. ALLOW within the mandate, ESCALATE above the condition so a human decides, DENY anything else. Every refusal reaches the authority.
