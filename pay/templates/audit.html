Evidence trail

Every filing, admission, signature, lifecycle change, verification and incident is one line in a hash chain: hash = sha256(prev_hash + entry). Verifications also carry a receipt signed by the bank. Any verification can be replayed from its stored inputs, including the bank's ledger total at the time, and must give the same answer. This is what a bank shows when a payment is disputed, and what a supervisor can request for an incident.
