Each proposed action is signed by the agent and presented with its passport. The gateway runs the ordered checks and returns ALLOW, ESCALATE or DENY with the rule, a reason, and a signed receipt. A copied passport, a wrong client, an out-of-scope action, or a suspended credential all stop here.

What the gateway sees on the passport

Permission, not personal data. No client list, no accountable person. Client authorisation is confirmed against the authority's records by reference.
